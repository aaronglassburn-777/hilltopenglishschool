Our Story

An extraordinary education, built on care

Hilltop English School was founded by Palita — known to her students as Teacher Mind — who earned first-class honors in her Bachelor of Arts in English from Mae Fah Luang University, and returned to Nanglae with a mission: give local children the kind of English education that opens doors.

"When children learn through play, they don't just remember English — they fall in love with it."

Every lesson at Hilltop is interactive. Games, fairy tales, songs and dancing turn listening, speaking, reading and writing into adventures children look forward to — guided by experienced, qualified teachers, including native English speakers.

What We Teach

Four skills. One joyful journey.

Our curriculum builds complete English ability — step by step, at every age — so children grow confident in every way they use the language.

👂

Listening

Songs, stories and natural conversation train young ears to understand real English from day one.

💬

Speaking

Games and role-play give every child the confidence to speak up — with native-speaker teachers to guide them.

📚

Reading

From phonics to fairy tales, children discover the joy of reading English on their own.

✏️

Writing

Creative, hands-on activities turn writing practice into self-expression children are proud of.
